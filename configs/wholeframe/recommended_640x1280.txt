Recommended settings for your current frame folders:

Source frames: 1280x720 images
Training size: 640x1280
Preprocess: center_crop
Reason: HNeRV default stride product is 5*4*4*2*2 = 320. 640 and 1280 are both divisible by 320, giving a 16x2x4 embedding when emb_dim=16.

Recommended command options:
  --img_size 640 1280
  --preprocess center_crop
  --enc_strides 5 4 4 2 2
  --dec_strides 5 4 4 2 2
